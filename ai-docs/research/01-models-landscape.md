## 1. Full songs with vocals (text-to-music)

### Closed services

- **R-001** Suno v6 launched 2026-09-09 as three models: v6 and v6-wild (Pro and Premier) and v6-mini (everyone). It edits part of a song from plain-language instructions, changes a single lyric, builds mashups, and takes text, audio, image and video input. Suno says it will "retire our previous models" as v6 rolls out. It names Warner Music Group, BMG and Believe as partners. Max length and training data are not stated. `[P]` https://blog.suno.com/blog/introducing-v6 (checked 2026-10-17)
- **R-002** Suno plans: Free $0 (50 credits/day, v6-mini only, no commercial use, 7 lifetime trial downloads), Pro $8/month (2,500 credits, 20 downloads/month, commercial use, 2 stem types), Premier $24/month (10,000 credits, 60 downloads/month, 3 stem types, Suno Studio, which has no download cap). Extra downloads can be bought. `[P]` https://suno.com/pricing; `[P]` https://blog.suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos (2026-08-10)
- **R-003** Suno has no public developer API. Every "Suno API" on the web is an unofficial wrapper, which the new terms (no outputs obtained outside Suno's download channels) make risky. `[K]` plus the terms in R-090.
- **R-004** Udio switched off audio, video and stem downloads after the UMG settlement (2025-10-29); a 48-hour export window ran 2025-11-03 to 05. Its help centre still said downloads were disabled in 2026, and the licensed streaming platform had not launched by late September 2026. `[S]` https://www.musegen.ai/blog/udio-review-2026, https://undetectr.com/blog/udio-download; the settlement itself `[P]` https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20251029EN10848. Udio's own pages would not render for the fetch tool.
- **R-005** Gemini API music models (page updated 2026-10-01): `lyria-3-clip-preview` always makes a 30 s clip (MP3); `lyria-3.5` makes full songs of "a couple of minutes", with vocals, timed lyrics and full arrangements, MP3 by default or WAV via `response_format`. Both are 44.1 kHz stereo and take text plus up to 10 images. All output carries a SynthID watermark. Requests for specific artist voices or copyrighted lyrics are blocked. Generation is single-turn, with no iterative editing. `[P]` https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/music-generation
- **R-006** Lyria prices (paid tier only; no free tier): Lyria 3.5 $0.08 per song, Lyria 3 Clip $0.04 per clip, Lyria 3 Pro Preview $0.08 per song. `[P]` https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing (2026-10-17). Lyria 3 reached the Gemini app in Feb 2026; Lyria 3.5 went to Flow Music on 2026-07-29 and to the Gemini app and API on 2026-09-04. `[S]` https://tbreak.com/google-lyria-3-5-gemini-music-generation/
- **R-008** ElevenLabs Music: v2 (launched 2026-05-26 per the company's posts, `[S]` dates vary) and v2.5 (live 2026-09-11, `model_id="music_v2_5"`; v2 stays available) `[P]` https://elevenlabs.io/docs/it/overview/capabilities/music.md. Features: vocals, section inpainting, reference audio, long-form composition, stems (2/4 on Starter and Creator, up to 6 on Pro). Plans: Free personal only; Starter $6 commercial with no streaming; Creator $22 and Pro $99 commercial, not enterprise. Self-serve commercial rights exclude film, TV and studio games, which need Enterprise. ElevenLabs says the model was trained on licensed data and is "cleared for broad commercial use". `[P]` https://elevenlabs.io/music
- **R-009** ElevenLabs API: Music $0.15 per minute, Sound Effects $0.12 per minute. `[P]` https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api (2026-10-17)
- **R-010** MiniMax hosted music: Music 2.5 (2026-01-28) `[P]` https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-music-25; Music 2.6 with a Cover feature (2026-04-10) `[S]` https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32953636; Music 3.0 API at $0.15 per song and lyrics at $0.01 `[S]` https://mer.vin/news/minimax-music-3-is-open-weight-but-not-open-for-business/
- **R-025** Mureka sells Basic ($8/month annual, up to 400 songs, MP3) and Pro ($24/month, WAV, stems, reference audio, voice cloning), described as royalty-free with commercial rights. `[P]` https://www.mureka.ai/. The API is prepaid top-up, about $0.045 to $0.05 per song. `[S]` https://www.cometapi.com/ai-music-api-pricing/, https://apiframe.ai/guides/mureka-api-guide. Current model version not confirmed.
- **R-026** Google bought ProducerAI (formerly Riffusion), announced 2026-02-24; the team went to Google Labs and DeepMind, and the product runs on Lyria 3 with SynthID. `[S]` https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/google-acquires-ai-music-platform-and-suno-challenger-producerai, https://www.billboard.com/pro/google-acquires-producerai-ai-music-startup/. Riffusion's open hobby repo has been dormant since 2024-07 `[P]` (GitHub API).
- **R-027** Sonilo: text-to-music and video-to-music (up to 6 min of video) as ComfyUI partner nodes `SoniloTextToMusic` and `SoniloVideoToMusic` `[L]`; the Comfy docs say outputs carry commercial rights `[P]` https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/sonilo/video-to-music. Training on a Shutterstock catalogue is `[S]` (Dealroom).

### Open models

- **R-011** MiniMax Music 3 open weights (released the week of 2026-08-13): Global LLM 8B (from Qwen3-8B), Local LLM 0.6B, 2.4B flow-matching DiT, 123M Flow-VAE. Songs up to 5 min (9,000 frames), sung lyrics with section tags, captions for vocal gender, timbre and harmony. Output 32 kHz 16-bit stereo WAV. Text-to-music only (no cover or edit). Runtimes: SGLang-Omni, diffusers, ComfyUI. VRAM per card: under 24 GB full, about 22 GB with offload, 8 GB with layer streaming. No published benchmarks. `[P]` https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3
- **R-012** MiniMax Music 3 licence is the **MiniMax-Music3 Community Licence**, not CC BY-NC as one blog says: free use, modification and distribution; companies with over $20M yearly revenue need written permission; commercial products must show "MiniMax-Music3" prominently in the UI; services must keep safeguards against infringement; the acceptable-use list bans publishing machine-generated content "without clear disclosure", impersonation and military use. Output ownership is not addressed. `[P]` https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3/blob/main/LICENSE. It runs on the test PC through native nodes (`MiniMaxMusic3TextEncode`, `EmptyMiniMaxMusic3LatentAudio`) with fp16 (4.9 GiB) and int8 (2.5 GiB) DiTs and a 9.2 GiB int8 text encoder; dynamic VRAM loading avoided OOM; runs took 62 s to 13 min 17 s (settings not recorded). `[L]` 2026-08-27
- **R-013** ACE-Step 1.5: MIT licence (README and model card; older notes saying Apache 2.0 are wrong; that is the earlier ACE-Step repo's licence). The model card says "You can strictly use the generated music for commercial purposes." DiT variants base, sft and turbo (8 steps), plus XL 4B base/sft/turbo (2026-04-02; 12 GB minimum with offload, 20 GB recommended). LM planner 0.6B/1.7B/4B. Duration 10 s to 10 min. Task types Text2Music, Cover, Repaint on all; Extract (stems), Lego and Complete on base only. 50+ lyric languages; LoRA training (8 songs, about 1 h on a 12 GB 3090). Latest release v0.1.8 (2026-05-18); last commit 2026-08-29. `[P]` https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5, https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5, GitHub API
- **R-014** ACE-Step on the test PC: 2B turbo through the REST API takes 22 s for a 60 s track warm, 31 s for 120 s, peak 10.4 GB; XL-sft through ComfyUI peaks at 12.9 GB. ComfyUI native nodes `TextEncodeAceStepAudio1.5`, `EmptyAceStep1.5LatentAudio`, `ReferenceTimbreAudio`. `[L]` (earlier local measurements)
- **R-015** YuE2 (m-a-p): current line since 2026-09 (yue2-v0.1.6 on 2026-09-09; tech report 2026-09-26; agent skill yue2-music v1.2.0 adds instrumentals and covers 2026-09-25). Lyrics plus style prompt to full song through an editable ABC melody/chord score layer (SheetSage2 transcribes audio to ABC for covers). Code Apache 2.0; weights CC BY-NC 4.0 with a creator permission: personal users, creators and musicians may monetise outputs with no royalty; companies need a licence. Needs 24 GB VRAM BF16 on Linux; 48 kHz stereo. Original YuE moved to the `YuE-v1` branch. `[P]` https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE, https://huggingface.co/m-a-p
- **R-016** YuE2 is native in ComfyUI: `YuE2GenerateABC`, `YuE2GenerateMusic`, `EmptyYuE2LatentAudio`, `SheetSage2AudioToABC` are in `comfy_extras` here `[L]`. Its weights are not downloaded on the test PC, so 16 GB fit is untested. Claims that it beats Suno v5 (WildSongBench) or v6 are self-reported and reached here only through press. `[S]` https://comfyui-wiki.com/news/2026-09-11-yue2-3b. Community add-ons: FL YuE2 piano roll, a YuE2 LoRA trainer `[S]`.
- **R-017** HeartMuLa (heartlib): HeartMuLa-oss-3B (2026-01-14; "happy-new-year" build 2026-02-13), HeartCodec 48 kHz, HeartTranscriptor (Whisper-based lyric transcription), HeartCLAP; MuLaCover cover/remix model from reference audio or melody/chord MIDI (2026-09-16). Licence moved to Apache 2.0 on 2026-01-20. Default length 4 min; runs at real-time factor about 1.0; `--lazy_load` for one GPU; ComfyUI via a community node. The 7B "comparable to Suno" model is unreleased. `[P]` https://github.com/HeartMuLa/heartlib
- **R-018** DiffRhythm v1.2 (2025-05-09): Apache 2.0 code and weights; base about 95 s and needs 8 GB with `--chunked`; full about 285 s. DiffRhythm 2 (weights 2025-10-30) is Apache 2.0, semi-autoregressive, takes a reference WAV; repo quiet since 2025-11. `[P]` https://github.com/ASLP-lab/DiffRhythm, https://huggingface.co/ASLP-lab/DiffRhythm2
- **R-019** Tencent SongGeneration / LeVo: the official `tencent-ailab/SongGeneration` repo returns 404 through the GitHub API today, and the HF repo returned 401 to the fetch tool. SongGeneration v2 (LeVo 2) was released around 2026-03-01 `[S]` https://gaga.art/blog/songgeneration-2/. The ComfyUI wrapper `smthemex/ComfyUI_SongGeneration` (updated 2026-09-28) supports v2 and GGUF, targets over 12 GB and warns that large may OOM on 12 GB `[P]` (wrapper README). Licence: treat as unknown until the LICENSE file is read.
- **R-020** SongBloom (NeurIPS 2025): the official `Cypress-Yang/SongBloom` repo is gone (GitHub 404); only mirrors and ComfyUI packs remain (`xuchenxu168/SongBloom_ComfyUI`). Licence reports conflict. `[P]` GitHub API; `[S]` https://huggingface.co/rtikw/SongBloom. Not recommended as a dependency.
- Also noted, not evaluated: Muse (open long-form song model trained on 116k songs, arXiv 2601.03973) `[P]` https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.03973.

## 2. Instrumental, BGM, loops, stems; real-time

- **R-021** Stable Audio 3.0 (2026-05-20): a family trained on fully licensed data (AudioSparx plus Freesound CC). Small (459M), Small-SFX, Medium (1.4B) and Large (2.7B). Small, Small-SFX and Medium are open weights; Large is API and enterprise self-host only. Small up to 2 min; Medium and Large up to 6 min 20 s. Instrumental and SFX, no vocals. `[P]` http://stability.ai/news-updates; sizes and lengths `[S]` https://artintech.substack.com/p/stable-audio-3-explained-in-5-figures. Inference code at `Stability-AI/stable-audio-3` (MIT, pushed 2026-10-11) `[P]`. A DAW plugin followed on 2026-08-18 `[P]`.
- **R-022** Stability Community Licence: free for organisations under $1M annual revenue, Enterprise above; "you own outputs" (between you and Stability); covers Stable Audio 3.0. `[P]` https://stability.ai/license. ComfyUI repackage `Comfy-Org/stable-audio-3` ships six checkpoints (medium, medium_base, small_music, small_music_base, small_sfx, small_sfx_base) plus `t5gemma_b_b_ul2` text encoder, licence tag `stable-audio-community` `[P]` https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/stable-audio-3. Native per the ComfyUI 2026-05-20 changelog `[L]`. Stable Audio Open 1.0 (47 s) remains a native template `[P]` https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/audio/stable-audio/stable-audio-1. The Stability partner API nodes (Stable Audio 2.5) are documented but absent from the local 0.38.2 install `[L]`.
- **R-023** MusicGen (AudioCraft): code MIT, weights CC BY-NC 4.0; 3.3B large; trained 2023. Non-commercial, so a poor default for a plugin. `[P]` https://huggingface.co/facebook/musicgen-large; repo pushed 2026-03-03 `[P]`.
- **R-024** Magenta RealTime 2 (MRT2): `mrt2_small` 230M and `mrt2_base` 2.4B; real-time streaming on Apple Silicon (small on any M-series, base needs Max-class); offline inference on NVIDIA through the Python library; JAX and MLX backends; text-prompted (MusicCoCa style model, SpectroStream codec), `jam` app with note control. Code Apache 2.0, weights CC BY 4.0, "Google claims no rights in outputs". v1 moved to `v1_legacy`. `[P]` https://github.com/magenta/magenta-realtime, https://huggingface.co/google/magenta-realtime-2. Fits an Apple Silicon Mac better than an NVIDIA PC.
- **R-007** Lyria RealTime (`models/lyria-realtime-exp`, experimental, page updated 2026-09-17): instrumental only, raw 16-bit stereo PCM at 48 kHz (one JS sample uses 44.1 kHz) as a streaming session, BPM 60 to 200, density and brightness 0 to 1, scale enum; changing BPM or scale needs a context reset. No price on the page. `[P]` https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/realtime-music-generation
- Stems from generators: ACE-Step `extract` (base model) `[P]` R-013; Suno 2 or 3 stem types by plan `[P]` R-002; ElevenLabs up to 6 stems `[P]` R-008; Mureka Pro stems `[P]` R-025. Loops need WAV or FLAC; MP3 encoder padding breaks seamless loops `[L]` (earlier local measurements).

## 3. Symbolic and MIDI generation

- **R-034** Magenta (classic TF repo) is archived (GitHub API, last push 2026-01-06). Do not build on it. `[P]`
- **R-035** Anticipatory Music Transformer (`jthickstun/anticipation`, Apache 2.0, pushed 2026-07-19): infilling and accompaniment over MIDI. MIDI-LLM (Llama-3.2-1B with the AMT's 55,030 MIDI tokens; Llama 3.2 Community Licence) does text-to-multitrack-MIDI and reports beating Text2midi. `[P]` GitHub API; MIDI-LLM details `[S]` https://huggingface.co/AEmotionStudio/midi-llm-models
- **R-036** Text2midi (AMAAI-Lab, MIT, 272M, AAAI 2025; repo idle since 2025-02) remains the common text-to-MIDI baseline. EleutherAI Aria (Apache 2.0, pushed 2026-05-12) is a piano MIDI model. `[P]` GitHub API; `[S]` https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/34516
- **R-037** YuE2's ABC score layer is the most practical "symbolic" route in 2026: generate or edit the ABC melody/chord plan, then render audio (R-015). MiniMax Music 3's section tags are "generative control rather than strict symbolic guarantees" `[S]`.
- An LLM writing MIDI or ABC/MusicXML directly (then rendering with FluidSynth or MuseScore) needs no model at all; that is a design option, not a finding. `[K]`

## 4. Editing: cover, remix, repaint, extend, separation, voice

- **R-028** Cover and repaint: ACE-Step 1.5 (`cover`, `repaint` by time range, `audio_cover_strength`; `complete` and `lego` on base) `[P]` R-013; YuE2 zero-shot covers from a transcribed score `[P]` R-015; HeartMuLa MuLaCover `[P]` R-017; MiniMax 2.6 Cover (API) `[S]` R-010; Suno v6 part-song edits and single-lyric changes `[P]` R-001; ElevenLabs section inpainting `[P]` R-008. Lyria 3.5 has no iterative editing `[P]` R-005.
- **R-030** Demucs: `facebookresearch/demucs` is archived (last push 2024-04-24); the author's fork `adefossez/demucs` (MIT) is maintained, last push 2026-08-31. `[P]` GitHub API
- **R-031** RoFormer family leads vocal separation: BS-RoFormer won SDX'23 `[P]` https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.02612; MVSEP reports its 2025-07 BS-RoFormer vocal model at 11.89 dB SDR on its own Multisong set and ships a 6-stem "BS Roformer SW" (vocals, bass, drums, guitar, piano, other) `[S]` https://mvsep.com/news/64 (self-reported, not a neutral benchmark). Local tools: ZFTurbo Music-Source-Separation-Training (MIT, pushed 2026-10-15), `python-audio-separator` (MIT, 2026-10-01), UVR GUI (MIT, 2026-10-06). `[P]` GitHub API
- **R-032** Meta SAM Audio (small, base, large, plus `-tv` variants): separates any sound from text, visual masks or time spans; music and instrument subjective scores 3.6 to 4.5 (scale undefined); SAM Licence; checkpoints gated on HF. `[P]` https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-audio
- **R-033** Voice conversion: RVC WebUI (MIT, pushed 2026-08-04) and Applio (MIT, active 2026-10-17) are the maintained routes; Seed-VC (GPL-3.0) is archived since 2025-04-20. `[P]` GitHub API. Converting a vocal into a real singer's voice without consent breaks Spotify, YouTube and MiniMax licence rules (R-093, R-094, R-012).
- **R-038** Singing voice synthesis: DiffSinger (openvpi, Apache 2.0, pushed 2026-10-16) is the active open route `[P]`; SoulX-Singer (zero-shot SVS, 2026-02, Apache 2.0, arXiv 2602.07803) `[S]` https://huggingface.co/AlexthePromptCoder/SoulX-Singer. Commercial: Synthesizer V, ACE Studio, Vocaloid `[K]`, versions not checked.
- **R-039** Lyrics alignment and transcription: WhisperX (BSD-2, pushed 2026-09-26) for word timestamps `[P]`; HeartTranscriptor for sung lyrics `[P]` R-017; ACE-Step bundles an `acestep-lyrics-transcription` skill `[L]` (earlier local measurements).

## 5. Audio-to-text and analysis

- **R-040** Transcription: MuScriptor (arXiv 2607.08168, 2026-07-10; 60M/100M/300M/1.3B decoder-only; 128 GM instruments in 36 groups; GitHub `muscriptor/muscriptor`; credited to Mirelo and Kyutai on mirrors) reports beating YourMT3+ by a wide margin (Dagstuhl ChoirSet frame F1 51.0 to 80.7); weights licence not stated in the paper. `[P]` https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.08168. MT3 (Apache 2.0, pushed 2026-10-07) and Spotify basic-pitch (Apache 2.0, last push 2025-11) remain the simple baselines; YourMT3 is GPL-3.0 and idle since 2024-11. `[P]` GitHub API
- **R-041** Beat, tempo, key: beat_this (CPJKU, MIT, ISMIR 2024, pushed 2026-05-28) for beats and downbeats; Essentia (AGPL-3.0, pushed 2026-10-14) `RhythmExtractor2013` for BPM, plus key extraction; Essentia is not real-time. `[P]` https://essentia.upf.edu/tutorial_rhythm_beatdetection.html, GitHub API. `all-in-one` (structure, beats, BPM; MIT) idle since 2024-05 `[P]`. AGPL matters if musicwright ever bundles Essentia.
- **R-042** Captioning and tagging: NVIDIA Music Flamingo (ICLR 2026; Audio Flamingo 3 backbone; about 15 min input; trained on MF-Skills, about 2M songs) reports beating prior models on music captioning and Q&A `[P]` https://research.nvidia.com/labs/adlr/MF. Qwen3-Omni beats GPT-4o-Audio and Gemini 2.5 Pro on MTG tagging, while specialist MuQ-MuLan still scores higher `[P]` https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.17765. ACE-Step's LM can caption ("understand") audio, and acestep.cpp exposes `/understand` `[L]` (earlier local notes).
- Chord recognition: no 2026 primary source found; Chordino (Vamp) and BTC are the usual open options `[K]`.

## 9. Legal and policy landscape

### Label litigation and settlements

- **R-080** UMG settled with Udio on 2025-10-29 and the two are building a licensed, opt-in creation and streaming platform; outputs stay on the platform. `[P]` https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20251029EN10848. Warner settled with Udio in Nov 2025 `[S]`.
- **R-081** Warner settled with Suno and partnered (Suno post 2025-11-25) `[P]` https://blog.suno.com/. Suno then signed BMG (2026-08-12) `[P]` and Believe/TuneCore (2026-09-08): tracks made with Suno's "industry partner model" may be distributed through Believe and TuneCore, which had blocked Suno output in April 2026 `[S]` https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-inks-global-licensing-deal-with-believe. Suno raised $400M at a $5.4B valuation (2026-06-03) `[P]` blog.
- **R-082** UMG and Sony are still suing Suno (D. Mass.); they sought to grow the work list from 560 to 61,026; a magistrate refused them Suno's Warner settlement in April 2026. One tracker says the schedule was reset on 2026-06-30 with dispositive motions due 2027-04-09, which would put a US fair-use ruling in 2027. Sony alone still sues Udio and filed a second SDNY suit on 2026-07-20 over 30,117 recordings. `[S]` https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-fights-to-keep-warner-music-settlement-terms-away-from-umg-and-sony/, https://dynamoi.com/learn/ai-music-distribution/ai-music-copyright-cases-timeline. Verify on the dockets before quoting.
- **R-083** GEMA v Suno, Munich Regional Court I, 2026-07-31 (42 O 763/25): largely for GEMA on both training and outputs of compositions; Suno conceded YouTube stream-ripping for training data; commentators call it the first EU ruling to attach liability to training done outside the EU. Full reasons were not public when reported. `[S]` https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmszvaaz6003oug849s2sqawl/gema-v-suno-ai-key-eu-decision-on-ai-training-outputs-and-copyright. GEMA v OpenAI (lyrics, 2025-11-11) went GEMA's way and is under appeal `[P]` https://www.gema.de/en/w/landmark-ruling-gema-against-openai
- **R-084** Licensing deals beyond Suno/Udio: UMG + Stability (2025-10-30) and WMG + Stability (2025-11-19); UMG and WMG joined Stability's Series B (2026-08-25) `[P]` http://stability.ai/news-updates. UMG + ElevenLabs multi-year deal for a separate licensed fan platform (2026-09-10) `[S]` https://au.variety.com/2026/music/news/umg-elevenlabs-ai-powered-music-platform-licensing-40165/. Spotify + UMG AI covers and remixes as a paid Premium add-on (announced 2026-05-21, no launch date) `[S]` https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-and-universal-music-group-strike-landmark-deal-to-let-fans-create-ai-covers-and-remixes-as-a-paid-premium-add-on/

### Copyright in outputs

- **R-085** US Copyright Office: Part 2 (Copyrightability, 2025-01-29) holds that purely AI-generated material is not protectable and prompts alone are not enough; human selection, arrangement and modification can be. Part 3 (training) is still a pre-publication version of 2025-05-09; nothing newer on the AI page. `[P]` https://www.copyright.gov/ai/ (Part 2 conclusion `[K]`, from the report itself, not re-read today)
- **R-086** The Supreme Court denied certiorari in Thaler v. Perlmutter (No. 25-449) on 2026-03-02, leaving the D.C. Circuit's human-authorship holding in place. `[S]` https://www.bakerdonelson.com/supreme-court-denies-certiorari-in-thaler-v-perlmutter-ai-cannot-be-an-author-under-the-copyright-act
- Consequence for musicwright: "you own the output" in a licence (Suno paid, Stability, ACE-Step) is a contract term between user and vendor; it does not create a copyright the user can enforce. Human-written lyrics and human edits are where protectable authorship lives. `[K]` reading of R-085.

### Vendor terms that bind outputs

- **R-090** Suno terms (revised 2026-08-10, effective 2026-09-03): Pro/Premier get Suno's rights in Output assigned, still subject to the terms and only for permitted downloads within the monthly cap; free tier is personal and non-commercial; remixes are jointly owned and non-commercial on every tier; recording or stream-ripping is banned; Suno may add fingerprints, watermarks or metadata showing tier and download status, and removing them is banned; users grant Suno a perpetual licence to their content, including to train models; Suno makes no promise that copyright exists in any Output. `[P]` https://suno.com/legal/terms
- **R-091** Google: SynthID on all Lyria output; artist-voice and copyrighted-lyric prompts blocked `[P]` R-005. ElevenLabs self-serve excludes film, TV and studio games `[P]` R-008. MiniMax Music 3 needs on-product attribution and AI disclosure `[P]` R-012. YuE2 weights allow creator monetisation but not company use without a licence `[P]` R-015.

### Platform and distributor rules

- **R-092** Spotify (2025-09-25 post): vocal impersonation only with the artist's authorisation; a spam filter that tags and stops recommending mass uploads, duplicates and too-short tracks; 75M spam tracks removed in 12 months; support for the DDEX AI-credit standard, with disclosure stated not to cause down-ranking. An April 16 update (year not shown, 2026 by context) launched an AI-credits beta in Song Credits with "tens of thousands" of credits submitted daily. `[P]` https://newsroom.spotify.com/tag/policy
- **R-093** YouTube: creators must disclose realistic AI content, explicitly including AI music that is the main focus of a video, through "AI use" in YouTube Studio; music gets a player label; YouTube may auto-label from C2PA metadata or detection; disclosure alone does not limit reach or monetisation; repeat non-disclosure can mean removal or YPP suspension. Monetisation still depends on the separate inauthentic/mass-produced content rules `[S]`. `[P]` https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- **R-094** Steam: the Content Survey's AI section covers content that "ships with your game, and is consumed by players", which includes sound, split into Pre-Generated (judged like any content) and Live-Generated (also describe guardrails). Development-efficiency tools are not the focus. `[P]` https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey. The Jan 2026 revision that narrowed scope is `[S]` https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-updates-AI-disclosure-form-requiring-developers-to-report-visible-and-in-game-AI-but-not-background-tools.1206103.0.html. AI-generated game music therefore needs a disclosure line.
- **R-095** Deezer (2026-07-21): AI tracks passed 50% of daily uploads (about 90,000 a day in June 2026); tagging since June 2025; AI music is 1 to 3% of streams and 85% of those are flagged as fraud and demonetised; Deezer will remove fraud-linked AI tracks and ones unstreamed for six months. `[P]` https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/
- **R-096** Distributors: DistroKid accepts AI music with rights and asks for disclosure; CD Baby rejects fully AI-generated works; TuneCore reportedly accepts only licensed-model output and now distributes Suno partner-model tracks (R-081). `[S]` https://dynamoi.com/learn/lists/distributors-that-accept-ai-music. Check each help centre at release time.
- **R-097** EU AI Act Article 50: disclosure duties apply from 2026-08-02; the machine-readable marking duty for synthetic audio (Art. 50(2)) was pushed to 2026-12-02 for systems already on the market by a provisional AI Omnibus deal (May 2026) that still needed formal adoption. `[S]` https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmoveo6ke00icuxskpgueb0od/ai-omnibus-update, https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article50-watermarking-deadline/

## 10. What this means for musicwright (first read)

- Local default: **ACE-Step 1.5** (MIT, commercial outputs, measured fast here, cover/repaint/extract, REST API and native ComfyUI). Second local engine: **MiniMax Music 3** (native, on disk, commercial under $20M with UI attribution and disclosure). **YuE2** for score-controlled songs and covers, flagged non-commercial for companies and untested on 16 GB. **Stable Audio 3** for instrumentals, loops and SFX (licensed training data, $1M threshold).
- Hosted fallbacks with real APIs: Lyria 3.5 ($0.08/song, SynthID), ElevenLabs Music ($0.15/min, licensed data), MiniMax API. Suno and Udio have no official API and should be documented, not automated.
- Every route should write a provenance sidecar (model, version, licence, seed, prompt, date) so disclosure on YouTube, Spotify (DDEX), Steam and under EU Art. 50 is one copy-paste.
