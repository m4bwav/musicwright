# Decision: tuning, repetition and section boundaries in `mw.py analyze`

Date: 2026-10-23. Status: adopted on branch books-11 (not released).

## Context

Track 8 left three measures from DeSantis, *Making Music*, unbuilt because librosa was missing:
tuning drift, repetition, and section boundaries from a reference (`analyze --sections`). librosa
1.0.0 is now installed on the owner's machine. Every threshold was set on real material before it
shipped. The AI set is 111 renders and layered parts on the owner's machine (ACE-Step 1.5,
MiniMax Music 3, film scores, lego layers and stage mixes). The human set is 11 recordings from
Wikimedia Commons, public domain or CC BY. It holds four Musopen Beethoven recordings, two Bach
cello suite movements, a 1916 Joplin piano roll, a 1920 dance band and three Kevin MacLeod pieces. The downloads
stayed in the session's scratch folder; the repository holds none of them.

## Rulings

- N1. librosa stays an optional import. AGENTS.md already allows it in `analyze` and `transcribe`
  only, with the `uv run` one-liner when it is missing. CI does not install it. Without it the
  new tests skip and the soft-failure test runs; `MUSICWRIGHT_REQUIRE_LIBROSA=1` makes them run
  (green here on Python 3.14.6, librosa 1.0.0, numpy 2.5.2). librosa on six CI jobs would add a
  heavy dependency to a CI whose Ubuntu leg already hangs in apt. The measures are pointers, not
  gates.
- N2. Tuning is reported three ways per file. `cents` is the circular mean against A440 over
  10 s windows, skipping windows 20 dB under the file. `spread_cents` is the 10th to 90th
  percentile, and `trend_cents` the median of the last third minus the first. Spread is never
  flagged. On a blues flute take the windows scatter about 20 cents either way and pyin agrees:
  those are bends, not drift. A dub bed with pitch-swept echoes spreads 42 cents.
- N3. OFF-A440 (an offset of 15 cents or more) is a layering warning, not a defect. It fires on
  6 of 111 AI files and on 4 of 11 human recordings. The human ones are string players tuned
  sharp (+18 to +20 cents) and a 1920 dance band (+33). It matters only when an A440 part joins.
- N4. DRIFT (trend of 15 cents or more) needs 9 windows (90 s). On 60 s files the trend was an
  intro or a bend (one kept flute take read -21 from its breathy first window). It fires on no
  real file, AI or human, not even the 1920 band; only a synthetic step proves it.
- N5. TUNING-GAP: with several files, each after the first reports `cents_from_first`, flagged
  at 15 cents. On real lego layers against the kept flute lead the gaps were 1 to 14 cents; a
  separately rendered jazz flute sits 28 cents from it.
- N6. REPEATS: `repetition.share` is the part of the track that repeats an 8 s block heard at
  least 8 s earlier, at chroma and MFCC similarity 0.9 or more. The flag needs a share of 0.9 on a
  track of 60 s or more. Human recordings top out at 0.61, a scherzo with its written repeats. On
  the AI set it fires on 5 of 111, all of them 10-minute ambient beds (0.97 to 0.98, one block of
  14 to 16 s). A loop's first pass never counts, so a short loop cannot reach 0.9.
- N7. Sections: a novelty curve (checkerboard kernel 24 s wide, peaks over 0.12 at least 12 s
  apart) sets the count, then librosa's agglomerative clustering places the boundaries, and each
  boundary moves to the nearest detected beat. The test set was 24 composites spliced from real
  renders with 2 s crossfades, 62 known boundaries. With the count given, agglomerative found 85%
  of the splices between different renders within 3 s; novelty peaks alone found 65% to 72%. The
  automatic count also found 85%, with about 0.7 sections too many on average. Splices between
  pieces of one render were found 12% to 19% of the time, and many of them are not musical
  boundaries at all. An eval run noticed boundaries on fractional bars, which led to the beat
  snap.
- N8. `--sections` takes nothing (automatic count), a number, or tags joined by `|` (the count
  and the names), so a `gen --sections` string can be passed as it is. Bars assume 4/4 and the
  tempo estimate, which can be doubled or halved.
- N9. Tempo, key and onset keep the file's own rate as before; the new measures run at 22050 Hz,
  the rate they were calibrated at. Loading everything at 22050 moved one render's tempo from
  156.6 to 117.5 bpm.

## Not built, and why

- Comparing found boundaries with the requested sections: `--sections` on `gen` carries tag
  names, not lengths, so there are no requested times to compare with. Passing the tags to
  `analyze --sections` names the found sections in order instead.
- A flag for wide pitch spread: bends, vibrato and effects cause it (N2).
- Drift between renders of one recipe: the OFF-A440 and TUNING-GAP numbers cover the use the
  book names (layering takes that agree).

## Cost

Installed skills 424 KB to 433 KB, still yellow (green line 400, yellow to 800).
